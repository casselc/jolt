## Conflict policy

- Preserve both upstream v0.8.6 CLI/build behavior and the aspect-only
  introspection, selection, weaving, reporting, and gates.
- Treat generated Chez and Gambit seeds as derived output: bootstrap from the
  release seeds, then remint them from the resolved source with Chez 10.4.1.
- The build host boundary carries optional target, target-pack, boot-mode, and
  aspect-config values in that order. Gates must exercise boot modes and a
  non-nil aspect selection together with ordinary builds.
- Keep the earlier aspect root and its parent in the lock. Schema 2 records the
  current upstream release separately and requires both histories in every
  canonical descendant.

Exact remint, local/hosted gate, independent-review, merge, and consumer-pin
evidence is recorded in the owning `chucklehead-dev/jolt-aspect-packs` ledger
when this epoch is published.
