# Jolt proposal proof custody — 2026-07-26

This record answers a narrower question than the architecture RFD: where does
each proof, model, oracle generator, and executable semantic companion live?
An artifact is considered durable here only when it is committed in the
repository that owns the modeled behavior and is reachable from a named
branch. A worktree path alone is not custody.

These records are bounded evidence, not blanket correctness certificates.
Every solver model retains its stated domain and assumptions, and every pure
Ansatz theorem retains the runtime and extraction trust boundaries recorded
beside it.

`jolt-bytes`, `jolt-bencode`, and the nREPL proposal are ordinary top-level Git
repositories, not hidden worktrees. They do not yet have publication remotes;
their checkpoints are local custody until a repository/account decision is
made. The upstream-owned nREPL proposal must not be published without a
separate authorization.

## Worktree disposition

The former Jolt-core branches `spike/ansatz-window-oracle` at `6d59af2d` and
`spike/ansatz-cursor-oracle` at `e970912e` are retained as original provenance.
They are no longer required to reproduce or consume the proof artifacts:
`jolt-bytes` contains the generators and exact generated bytes, and its sync
gate rejects any divergence between generator output and runtime fixtures.
Those spike branches should not be merged into core; byte-contract proof
custody belongs with the byte package.

The research-only AOT v5 runtime prototype is likewise not a missing proof
branch. Its negative result remains research, while every AOT formal model and
the corresponding proof prose are already on
`codex/upstream-improvements-6-8`.

The old `jolt-net` W2 and W3 review worktrees are historical review anchors.
Their accepted content is reachable from the W5 branch above; the rejected W1
target relabeling commit contains no unique proof model. Windows W4/W5 evidence
is committed and remotely reachable at the named checkpoint rather than being
owned by the `D:` worktrees used to obtain native evidence.

## Reproduction boundary

The portable proof packages expose repository-local entry points:

```sh
cd jolt-bytes
bin/verify-ansatz
bin/verify-oracle-sync
bin/test-jvm
bin/jnc -M:test
bin/jnc -M:hegel
bin/verify-copy-models

cd ../jolt-bencode
bin/verify-ansatz
bin/verify-models
bin/test-jvm
bin/jnc -M:test
bin/jnc -M:hegel
```

The Ansatz commands require the JVM and Java 21 or newer. The production
libraries do not depend on Ansatz. SMT/Prolog models require their named solver
or Chiasmus; a missing solver must be reported as unverified rather than
silently skipped.

Network, TCP, HTTP, and Hegel reproduction commands and exact native platform
boundaries remain in their owning `docs/proofs/`, runtime, and platform
coverage documents. This index deliberately links to those records instead of
copying their claims into core.

No artifact in this custody map authorizes a push to `jolt-lang/jolt`, an
upstream pull request, or publication of an upstream-owned consumer branch.
